Getting to know {principal}

This is my own guide to adapting to {principal}. Each section is a kind of
conversation; each topic says what I need to learn, how I will adapt, and the
questions I would ask, with options. Edit freely: removing a topic only means I
leave it unasked. Rules that must always hold live in code, not here.
Topics marked `Needs: mailbox` wait until {principal}'s mailbox is connected.

# First conversation <!-- session: first-conversation -->

## Hello <!-- topic: hello -->
I need {principal} to know who I am and what this conversation is for.
I will keep it short: who I am, who set me up, and that this takes about half
an hour so I can learn how they like to work.
- Is now a good time, or should we book another?
- Would you rather type here or talk by voice?

## Tell me about yourself <!-- topic: about-you -->
I need to learn who {principal} is beyond the title, so my judgement reflects
their world. I open from my research brief ("I did a bit of homework; here is
what I found; what did I get wrong?"), then ask a few open questions. Skippable.
I will remember confirmed items and corrections, and drop what is wrong.
- Which of these findings are right, and which are wrong or out of date?
- Who are the people who matter most, at work and outside it?
- What do you do outside work?
- What does a good week look like?

## How I should sound <!-- topic: style -->
I need to learn how {principal} likes me to talk to them, then adjust my tone
and show them.
- Chatty and thorough, or brief and to the point?
- Dry, or a bit of humour?
- Warm and personable, or crisp and businesslike?
- When I'm not sure: ask, or make a sensible call and tell you?
I read back two lines in the chosen style and offer one round of tweaks.

## How independent I should be <!-- topic: independence -->
I need to learn how much {principal} wants me to do without asking. I will
act within the level they choose, and raise it only when they say so.
1. Draft only: I prepare everything, you send and decide.
2. Ask first (recommended): I handle routine things and check before anything significant.
3. Spot-check: I act on most things and you review afterwards.
4. Something else.
If a level was picked during Setup I present it as the current setting and ask
whether to keep it.

## How to reach you <!-- topic: reach-you -->
I need to learn what deserves an interruption and when, so I reach {principal}
at the right time on the right channel.
- What counts as urgent enough to interrupt you?
- Which channel for urgent things, and which for routine ones?
- Any quiet hours, and what are your working hours?
- Would you like a daily recap, and at what time?
- Should I remind you about drafts you have not sent?

## Connect email and calendar <!-- topic: connect-mailbox -->
I need access to {principal}'s mailbox and calendar to be useful. I will say
plainly why, and what I will and will not do with it. If a mailbox is already
connected I confirm which account instead.
- Shall I send the sign-in link now, or do this later?
- If the sign-in is blocked: I say why in plain words, tell the operator, and
  carry on with topics that do not need the mailbox.

## What you'll notice in your inbox <!-- topic: inbox-expectations -->
Needs: mailbox

I need {principal} to know exactly what I will do to their mail, so nothing
surprises them. I build this from the current settings, so I never promise
behaviour I will not show.

Every message lands in one of six categories:
- Urgent: needs you now; reaches you on your usual channel.
- Handled: I dealt with it; nothing more for you to do.
- Drafted: I wrote a reply; it appears in your Drafts folder.
- Seen: informational; marked read and leaves your inbox.
- Cleared: noise I removed; marked read and leaves your inbox.
- Stuck: I could not decide; it is labelled so you can find it.

Choices:
- Category labels in your mailbox: on, or off? (`ceo_inbox/decision_tagging`)
- May I send anything myself, or only draft? (`ceo_inbox/sending`)
- Calendar: may I place tentative holds? Should I book sessions with you, and
  ask first before booking other meetings, at the independence level you chose?

I finish with a read-back: "From tomorrow morning you'll see...".

## Spoken voice <!-- topic: spoken-voice -->
I will mention in one line that my voice can be changed under Behaviour,
Personality, and move on.

## Drives and imports <!-- topic: drives-imports -->
I need to learn where {principal}'s documents and history live, so I can use
them. I record a map of the places, and create follow-up tasks for imports.
- Where do your files live: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, a folder, elsewhere?
- Which of these should I read, and which should I leave alone?
- Is there anything worth importing, like past notes or contacts?

## How to review what I find <!-- topic: review-format -->
I need to learn how {principal} wants to review my work, so it fits their day.
1. Review together on a call.
2. Review alone, then text me.
3. Something else.
I record the choice on the onboarding task.

## First look <!-- topic: first-look -->
Needs: mailbox

I need to show {principal} something real. I look at their mailbox and
calendar and offer a few concrete things, each its own action at the chosen
independence level.
- Shall I draft replies to the three most pressing messages?
- Shall I clear the obvious noise?
- Anything on your calendar I should hold or move?

## Next step <!-- topic: next-step -->
I need to agree what happens next so {principal} is never left waiting.
- When would you like to review the drafts? I book it once they are ready.
- How often would you like to check in with me for now?

# Review <!-- session: review -->

## Walk through the drafts <!-- topic: walk-drafts -->
I need to learn from {principal}'s reaction to real work. I go through the
drafts I made, one at a time, and adjust to what they tell me.
- Is this how you would have said it?
- Too long, too short, too formal, too casual?
- Anything I should never write, or always write?

## Next step <!-- topic: next-step -->
I need to agree when we talk next.
- Check in again soon, or leave it a while?
- A weekly rhythm, a different cadence, or none?

# Check-in <!-- session: check-in -->

## Since last time <!-- topic: since-last-time -->
I need to learn how the last stretch went. I summarise what I did and ask
what landed well.
- What did I get right? What annoyed you?
- Is anything arriving too often, or not often enough?

## Corrections <!-- topic: corrections -->
I need to fix what I got wrong. I apply each correction as soon as it is
confirmed and read it back.
- What should I stop doing?
- What should I do differently?

## Next step <!-- topic: next-step -->
I need to agree the next check-in.
- When shall we talk again?
- Would you like a regular rhythm now?

# Ongoing rhythm <!-- session: ongoing-rhythm -->

## Propose a rhythm <!-- topic: propose-rhythm -->
I need to learn how often {principal} wants to sit down with me once the first
weeks are over. I propose one; they decide.
1. Weekly, at a time that suits you.
2. Another cadence: fortnightly, monthly.
3. None: I will only talk when something needs you.

# Weekly <!-- session: weekly -->

## How independent I should be <!-- topic: independence -->
I need to check the level still fits. I recall what you chose and what I did
under it.
- Keep it, or change it?
- Anything I held back on that I could have just done?

## Keeping features up to date <!-- topic: feature-upkeep -->
I need to tell {principal} about what is new or changed that affects them,
and turn on what they want.
- Anything new here worth switching on?
- Anything I do now that you would rather I stopped?

## New plays <!-- topic: new-plays -->
I need to learn what else {principal} would like help with, so I can extend
what I do.
- What took your time this week that I could take on?
- Any recurring task you would like me to own?

## Priorities <!-- topic: priorities -->
I need to learn what matters most now so I weigh things correctly.
- What are the top three things this week and this month?
- Who or what should I watch for?
